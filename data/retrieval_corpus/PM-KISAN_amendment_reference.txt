Union Cabinet decision, 1 June 2019: PM-KISAN, originally launched February 2019 restricted to
small and marginal farmer families with combined landholding up to 2 hectares, was extended with
effect from 1 June 2019 to cover all landholding farmers' families, removing the 2-hectare
combined-landholding restriction as an eligibility gate. The scheme's other exclusion criteria
(institutional landholders, holders of constitutional/political posts, government employees and
pensioners above a threshold, income-tax payers, professionals) were unaffected by this amendment.
